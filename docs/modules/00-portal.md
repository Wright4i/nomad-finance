# Module 0 — Portal

The signed-in app is a side menu and a main pane. Below the `md` breakpoint the menu is a drawer opened from the top bar. The menu is New Log, Planner, Income, Bills, Calendar, Trips, Envelopes, Accounts, and Settings. New Log sits above a light rule, larger, bold, centered, and white on the primary teal, with corners half as round as the other menu pills. A large gap sits under Accounts, then the account name, then Reset demo data when the signed-in user is Demo, then a light rule, then Settings immediately above Log out. Settings uses the same control styling as Log out. Income opens the payor list at `/pay-cycles`. Add Bucket on Envelopes opens a new bucket. The bucket list stays at `/categories`. Planner still links to Bills. Vehicles and exchange rates are reached from Settings. Accounts does not link to them. A skip link moves to the main pane. Demo's menu also has Reset demo data, which asks to reset the sample books before it restores them.

Login asks for a name and a password. Use demo signs in as Demo. New user sits beside Use demo, opens the same fields as setup, creates another user, and signs that user in. That user starts with empty accounts, bills, and rates, and with the vehicle and fuel price from that form. The owner's books and Demo's sample books stay as they were. Setup asks for a name, a password, and a base currency, plus the first vehicle and the fallback fuel price. The vehicle has a name, a kind, and a minimum and maximum miles per gallon, and it is starred because the fuel label uses that vehicle. The price is the same figure Settings stores, in the base currency. An empty price means miles only. The owner is the user created at setup. Demo is the sample user. Each user sees only their own accounts, bills, rates, buckets, pay cycles, and envelopes.

The app is dark by default. The theme color is `#101714`, and `#f4efe4` with the light theme. Settings stores a light theme and the fallback fuel price. Teal marks checking, Needs, rent, assets, and primary actions. Sky marks savings accounts, the Savings group, electricity, water, and paid bills. Lime marks cash, groceries, debt, and a truck. Coral marks credit cards, liabilities, restaurants, and healthcare. Rose marks loans, lodging, and an RV. Plum marks Wants and subscriptions. Stone marks other accounts, other bills, paused bills, a semi, and an irregular pay cycle. Internet and cellular are iris. Fuel and travel are amber. Mortgage is blossom. Storage is sea. Propane and repairs are apricot. Maintenance is fern. Insurance is tide. Roadside and emergency are poppy. Mailbox is lemon. Laundry and liquidities are lagoon. Memberships are violet. Pets are berry. Misc and investments are citrus. A vehicle bill and a car are sage. Entertainment is blush. Shopping shares plum with subscriptions. Weekly is teal, biweekly is sky, semimonthly is plum, and monthly is lime. Amber marks amounts left out of the net-worth total. Fraunces is used for the net worth figure and for group titles. Outfit is the rest of the UI. A mark sits beside each account type, bucket group, bill type, vehicle kind, and bill status. Amount color still follows side: teal for an asset, coral for a liability.

Planner, for the browser's local date sent as `today`:

The heading is the word Planner. When a stay is underway on that date and its city is known, the heading is an italic Fraunces phrase that names the city: Welcome to {city}, Hello from {city}, Settled in {city}, or Good to be in {city}. The same stay keeps the same phrase. The stay is the open stay, the first in arrival order whose dates cover today. A shared handoff date uses that earlier stay. No underway stay, or an underway stay with no city, leaves the word Planner in the usual type. The heading is a label. It does not move a balance, post, or change which stay is selected.

- Net worth in the base currency
- Asset total and liability total, converted
- Accounts that could not be converted, named, in their own currency
- Three gauges for the open period, each in a card. Needs is 50% of the positive amounts assigned, Wants is 30%, and Savings is 20%. A negative assignment is overspending and does not fill a gauge. The left side of a gauge is that share. Anything past it continues on the right in red and is labeled Over. Caps are gone. Opening a gauge lists that group's buckets and the amounts assigned in the current month. Available to assign is a card under the gauges, with the amount assigned, the pool, and Assign, which opens Envelopes. Planner does not step the assignment window. Current Month and Next Month stay on the Envelopes page. Cash with no rate stays off this block and remains under Left out of the total
- Bills for the current month or the next month, as a pie of bill types. On a wide screen the month switch is two stacked squares, the pie sits in the center, and the legend sits on the right. A narrow screen stacks those three. A slice is the due amount for that type: active bills whose next due date falls in that month. Paid is not charted. A type with no due amount is omitted. Each slice uses that type's color, and the legend names the type and the amount. When the due bills use more than one currency, each currency has its own pie. Mark paid still does not move a balance
- Upcoming Stay for each stay whose arrival is today, tomorrow, or the day after, soonest first. The card names the place and the arrival date and opens that stay. A stay that already arrived stays off the list, even while it is underway. The card is a reminder and does not move a balance or create a posting

`horizon_days` stays stored on the user and is unused on this screen. Calendar, at `/calendar`, is a month of stays, paydays, and bill due dates and latest paid dates. A stay is a sky box on each day from arrival through departure, with a small tree mark, the place name, and those dates. The day dialog shows that same tree mark. An unpaid occurrence is a coral box. A paid occurrence is a lilac box on the due date that was paid. A repeating bill keeps that paid date and shows the next due date as unpaid. Later months also show each date the series comes due, stepped forward from that next unpaid date. A projected date is unpaid, and a date that already holds the paid occurrence is not projected again. The box shows the name, a small mark for that bill type, and the amount as a negative. The day dialog names the bill type in text, shows that same mark, and colors the amount coral for an unpaid occurrence and lilac for a paid occurrence. A payday is a teal box with the payor, a small mark for the cycle length, and the paycheck preview as a positive. The day dialog shows that same length mark. Show Income, Show Stays, Show Paid Bills, and Show Unpaid Bills sit above the grid, checked, and hide that kind on the grid only. The browser's local today is marked on that date alone. The bar under the grid shows Income in teal, Bills in red, Paid in lilac, and Net in black, in the base currency. Paid is the sum of paid occurrences in that month. A bill in another currency is an extra red total and is not added into that net. Stays do not change those totals. The bar is a label. It does not list the fuel label. Previous month and Next month stay. A day opens that day's stays, then income, then bills, including an empty section. Below the md breakpoint the month is one full-width day per row, in date order, with the weekday on the day.

See [the calendar horizon assigns nothing](../adr/0017-calendar-horizon-assigns-nothing.md) and [a vehicle is its own odometer](../adr/0019-vehicle-odometer.md).

Empty planner copy points at creating an account. Bill screens point at creating an account when none exist.

Routes: `/` is Planner. Also `/calendar`, `/trips`, `/trips/new`, `/trips/:id`, `/envelopes`, `/log`, `/accounts`, `/accounts/new`, `/accounts/:id`, `/bills`, `/bills/new`, `/bills/:id`, `/categories`, `/categories/new`, `/categories/:id`, `/pay-cycles`, `/pay-cycles/new`, `/pay-cycles/:id`, `/settings`, `/settings/vehicles`, `/settings/vehicles/new`, `/settings/vehicles/:id`, `/settings/rates`. `/rates` opens the same exchange-rate screen. `/plans` and `/credit` return to `/`. Signed-out visitors see setup or login instead of the shell.

The menu stays those nine hubs. Module status is in [the module list](README.md). Themes beyond light and dark wait there too.
