# Module 0 — Portal

The signed-in app is a side menu and a main pane. Below the `md` breakpoint the menu is a drawer opened from the top bar. On a wide screen a collapse control narrows the menu to an italic N, a New log mark, the page icons, the account's initial, a round button showing the current night or day, and Log out. Pointing at one of those shows its name after a short pause. Clicking the round button switches to the other setting. The narrow menu is not used below the `md` breakpoint. The menu is New log, then Planner, Calendar, and Trips, then Bills, Budget, and Transfers, then Accounts, Income, and Vehicles. Transfers uses a shuffle mark of two crossing arrows. A light rule sits under Finance, under New log, under Trips, and under Transfers. New log is larger, bold, centered, and white on the primary teal, with corners half as round as the other menu pills. The footer sits at the bottom: the period dates, Previous and Next as one slanted pair, a light rule, the account name, Reset demo data when the signed-in user is Demo, then a night and day pill immediately above Log out. The pill is one rounded control split into a moon half and a sun half. The active half is teal with a black icon, and the other half is black with a white icon. Anyone who is not Demo opens their name there to change that name or password. Income opens the payor list at `/pay-cycles`. Add bucket on Budget opens a new bucket. The bucket list is Budget. `/buckets` opens Budget. Planner still links to Bills. Vehicles is a direct menu link. Accounts does not link to them. A skip link moves to the main pane. Demo's menu also has Reset demo data, which asks to reset the sample books before it restores them.

Login asks for a name and a password. Use demo signs in as Demo. New user sits beside Use demo when `FINANCE_NEW_USER=1`, opens the same fields as setup, creates another user, and signs that user in. Without that flag, login does not offer New user and another user is refused. The owner and Demo still sign in. Setup still creates the owner when there is none. That user starts with empty accounts and bills, and with the vehicle and fuel price from that form. The owner's books and Demo's sample books stay as they were. Setup asks for a name, a password, the first vehicle, and the fallback fuel price. The vehicle has a name, a kind, and a minimum and maximum miles per gallon, and it is starred because the fuel label uses that vehicle. The price is stored on that vehicle, in cents. An empty price means miles only. The owner is the user created at setup. Demo is the sample user. Each user sees only their own accounts, bills, buckets, pay cycles, budget, and calendar entries.

The app is dark by default. The theme color is `#101714`, and `#f4efe4` with the light theme. A sidebar pill stores the light or dark theme. Each vehicle stores its own fallback fuel price. Less motion from the system still softens what plays. Teal marks checking, Needs, rent, assets, and primary actions. Sky marks savings accounts, the Savings group, electricity, water, and paid bills. Lime marks cash, groceries, debt, and a truck. Coral marks credit cards, liabilities, restaurants, and healthcare. Rose marks loans, lodging, and an RV. Plum marks Wants and subscriptions. Stone marks other accounts, other bills, paused bills, a semi, and an irregular pay cycle. Internet and cellular are iris. Fuel, travel, and a utv are amber. Mortgage is blossom. Storage is sea. Propane and repairs are apricot. Maintenance and an atv are fern. Insurance is tide. Roadside and emergency are poppy. Mailbox and a golf cart are lemon. Laundry and liquidities are lagoon. Memberships are violet. Pets and a dirt bike are berry. Misc and investments are citrus. A vehicle bill and a car are sage. Entertainment is blush. Shopping shares plum with subscriptions. Weekly is teal, biweekly is sky, semimonthly is plum, and monthly is lime. Amber marks amounts left out of the net-worth total. Fraunces is used for the net worth figure, Available to assign, and group titles. The rest of the UI uses the system face. A mark sits beside each account type, bucket group, bill type, vehicle kind, and bill status. Amount color still follows side: teal for an asset, coral for a liability.

Planner, for the browser's local date sent as `today`:

The heading is the word Planner. When a stay is underway on that date and its city is known, the heading is an italic Fraunces phrase that names the city. Fifty phrases share that job. Each time the planner loads, one of them is chosen at random, and that visit keeps it. The stay is the open stay, the first in arrival order whose dates cover today. A shared handoff date uses that earlier stay. No underway stay, or an underway stay with no city, leaves the word Planner in the usual type. The heading is a label. It does not move a balance or change which stay is selected.

On a narrower desktop window, the sidebar collapses to icons on its own so the planner keeps room. The collapse control still overrides that until the window widens again. Net worth is assets minus liabilities, in cents, with every account included. It keeps the same handle and wrench as the widgets. The handle drags it on a wide screen, and its place is saved in `planner_order` as `net`. The figure is smaller than a full display size. That figure and Available to assign use the display face. Every other number uses the same face as the assets amount. Assets sits on the left under it, the word above its amount, left aligned, and that amount is teal. Liabilities sits on the right, the word above its amount, and that amount is right aligned in coral with a minus. The amount does not roll. Interest gained sits at the lower left in teal, and interest lost sits at the lower right in coral. Each amount is followed by /mn in that same color. When assets and liabilities stack on a narrower card, those labels hide and the same per-month figures move inline beside each total in parentheses, such as $56,690.75 (+$178.13/mn) and -$106,868.01 (-$253.85/mn). A dollar above zero is teal. A dollar below zero is coral and shows a minus. Zero may be grey or white. An amount field accepts a number or parentheses, plus, minus, times, and divide, and it resolves on blur and before save. A percent, a date, miles, and a count stay white or grey. An amount on the calendar may stay black, and it still shows a minus when it is negative. Text that finishes a dollar amount, such as /mn, uses that amount's color.

Seven blocks fill that screen. Each block matches the net worth height. The default order is Net worth beside Income, then Calendar, Trips, and Accounts in three columns, then Budget as one strip, then Bills. Extra content scrolls inside the block. On a phone those three columns stack. A handle on each widget drags it on a wide screen. On a phone that handle is hidden. The wrench opens Edit {name} layout, with Move back and Move forward. Move back swaps that widget one place earlier in `planner_order` (left on a wide screen, up when the widgets stack). Move forward swaps it one place later (right, or down when stacked). The first widget cannot move back. The last cannot move forward. The others reflow, and the order is saved on the user as `planner_order`. An unknown or partial order falls back to that default, and a widget is never dropped. That same wrench sets the widget's column span on the six-column grid. Net worth and each widget keep a span in `planner_spans`. The control is a slider from 1 to 6. On a phone the slider is locked, and the current span still shows. Income defaults to 3 beside net worth. Calendar, Trips, and Accounts default to 2. Budget and Bills default to 6. Anything else falls back to those spans. On a phone the row still stacks into one column. New log stays in the menu.

- Income shows the soonest payday: the payor, the date under the payor, and the amount in the display face. Still coming in sits on the bottom line, the paychecks in the accounting period dated today or later. A payday before today has been received and is left out. It links to Income. On that date the kicker reads Payday in teal and the line settles once. Any other day the line is still
- Calendar is titled Calendar. Three colored buttons at the upper right toggle stays, bills, and income on the miniature days; a tooltip names each filter. Calendar entries stay on. Span 1 or 2 shows one day. Span 3 or 4 shows two days. Span 5 or 6 shows three days. Those days start at today when today is inside the accounting period, and at the period start when it is not. A day past the period end is left off. They stay in calendar order, in one row that shares the widget width. Each day is the same square as that date on the Calendar page: the date pill, then that day's stays, calendar entries, paydays, and bills. Clicking a calendar entry opens it. Today keeps the teal ring. Three items stay in view, and the rest scroll. A day with no items still shows the date. Each day links to Calendar. On a phone the widget stacks to one column, and the day count still follows the span
- Trip is titled Trip. It leads with a stay that arrives today or tomorrow, ahead of a stay that is only underway. Otherwise it shows the stay whose dates cover today: place, city, and region, with the lodging mark. The day after tomorrow does not lead. The map is Carto Dark Matter, zoomed in on that stay, with no pin label, and it runs the width of the card under the handle and wrench. No current stay is one muted line linking to Trips. The cell opens that stay, or Trips when there is none. It does not move a balance
- Accounts is titled Accounts. It shows one column per account type that has an account: checking, savings, cash, credit, loan, fuel, and other. Each column is that type's total. An asset total is teal. An amount owed is coral and shows a minus. Empty types are skipped. A horizontal rule sits above the liabilities. The totals pack in a tight grid so the block does not scroll on a typical laptop. It links to Accounts. Totals only
- Budget is titled Budget. Needs, Wants, and Savings are the 50/30/20 comparison, each with the same resting gauge as a locked bucket on New Log: assigned against that group's share of the income still coming in plus those accounts, filled from the bottom. Above each bar is that group's assigned percent of goal, rounded. Each gauge names Assigned and Goal. On a wide window the three gauges share one row. On those narrower desktops the meters and type slim down. The three gauges stay on one row when the budget widget is wide enough; otherwise they wrap to two or one columns, with Assigned and Goal allowed to wrap instead of an ellipsis. Only when the budget widget itself is very narrow, such as a span of one, each gauge stacks vertically with a meter beside its readout. The net-worth figure scales with the card and stays clear of the handle and wrench instead of clipping with an ellipsis. There is no striped preview and no separate Assign button. Opening a row lists that group's buckets and the amounts assigned in the accounting period. Available to assign is one right-aligned line under the strip with that amount only. When the period on screen ended before today, that line is hidden and the widget says this period has closed. Planner does not step the assignment window
- Bills for the accounting period, as one pie of bill types, smaller than before, with its legend. The line under the title is that period's dates. A slice is every unpaid occurrence of an active bill in that period. Paid and paused stay out. A type with no due amount is omitted. Each slice uses that type's color, and the legend names the type and the amount, largest due first. Mark paid still does not move a balance. The wedges do not animate

The period in the sidebar sits between the previous and next arrows. Previous reaches 11 periods before the one that contains today, and Next reaches 11 periods after it. Collapsed, that line is the month and day only, as in 10/16-11/15, in very small type, and the previous and next arrows each sit on their own row under it. Its start and length are saved from the name dialog. A field tip lines up with the label's upper left and paints above that dialog. Planner, Bills, Budget, and Income follow it. Calendar, at `/calendar`, draws one standard calendar month and does not follow that period. Previous month and Next month move only that month. Stays, paydays, and bill due dates and latest paid dates are the ones in that month. Empty cells only line the first day up under its weekday. A stay is a sky box on each day from arrival through departure, with a small tree mark, the place name, and the city and state, such as Apache Junction, AZ. Those dates stay off that line. A stay with only a city or only a state shows that one. A stay with neither shows the arrival and departure, or Stay when those dates are the same day or cannot be read. The day dialog shows that same tree mark and that same line. An unpaid occurrence is a coral box. A paid occurrence is a lilac box on the due date that was paid. A repeating bill keeps that paid date and shows the next due date as unpaid. Later months also show each date the series comes due, stepped forward from that next unpaid date. A projected date is unpaid, and a date that already holds the paid occurrence is not projected again. The box shows the name, a small mark for that bill type, and the amount as a negative. The day dialog names the bill type in text, shows that same mark, and colors the amount coral for an unpaid occurrence and lilac for a paid occurrence. A payday is a teal box with the payor, a small mark for the cycle length, and the paycheck preview as a positive. The day dialog shows that same length mark. A calendar entry is a stone box on each day from its start through its end, with its text. Add entry, at the top right, opens that start date, end date, and text. The end is on or after the start. Clicking the box edits the text and the dates, or deletes the entry. Entries stay on the grid when income, stays, or bills are hidden, and they do not change the month totals. Each day shows at most three of those boxes. A day with more shows a down arrow, and that day's list scrolls so the rest can be reached. The day number stays at the top of the day while the list scrolls. Show Income, Show Stays, Show Paid Bills, and Show Unpaid Bills sit above the grid, checked, and hide that kind on the grid only. The browser's local today is marked on that date alone. The bar under the grid shows Income in teal, Debt in coral, Net, then a rule and italic Paid, and that amount stays grey. Debt is every unpaid occurrence of an active bill, as a positive amount. Weekly counts each date. Paused and archived stay off. Net is Income minus Debt. Paid is not subtracted again. A bill in another currency is an extra red total and is not added into that net. Stays do not change those totals. The bar is a label. It does not list the fuel label. Previous month and Next month stay. A day opens that day's stays, then income, then bills, including an empty section. Below the md breakpoint the month is one full-width day per row, in date order, with the weekday on the day.

Routes: `/` is Planner. Also `/calendar`, `/trips`, `/trips/new`, `/trips/:id`, `/budget`, `/log`, `/accounts`, `/accounts/new`, `/accounts/:id`, `/bills`, `/bills/new`, `/bills/:id`, `/buckets/new`, `/buckets/:id`, `/pay-cycles`, `/pay-cycles/new`, `/pay-cycles/:id`, `/vehicles`, `/vehicles/new`, `/vehicles/:id`. Vehicles lists the live vehicles. Show archived lists a hidden vehicle, and that row can delete it. Archive clears the star. Unarchive puts the name back and does not star it. Delete removes an archived vehicle. Its fuel entries stay, without that vehicle. `/buckets` opens Budget. `/settings`, `/settings/rates`, `/plans`, and `/credit` return to `/`. `/settings/vehicles` opens Vehicles. `/rates` opens the same exchange-rate screen. Signed-out visitors see setup or login instead of the shell.

The menu stays those ten hubs. Module status is in [the module list](README.md). Themes beyond light and dark wait there too.
