# Module 0 — Portal

The signed-in app is a side menu and a main pane. Below the `md` breakpoint the menu is a drawer opened from the top bar. The menu is New Log, then Planner, Calendar, and Trips, then Income, Bills, and Envelopes, then Accounts and Vehicles. A light rule sits under Finance, under New Log, under Trips, and under Envelopes. New Log is larger, bold, centered, and white on the primary teal, with corners half as round as the other menu pills. The footer sits at the bottom: a light rule, the account name, Reset demo data when the signed-in user is Demo, a light rule, then a night and day pill immediately above Log out. The pill is one rounded control split into a moon half and a sun half. The active half is teal with a black icon, and the other half is black with a white icon. Anyone who is not Demo opens their name there to change that name or password. Income opens the payor list at `/pay-cycles`. Add Bucket on Envelopes opens a new bucket. The bucket list stays at `/categories`. Planner still links to Bills. Vehicles is a direct menu link. Accounts does not link to them. A skip link moves to the main pane. Demo's menu also has Reset demo data, which asks to reset the sample books before it restores them.

Login asks for a name and a password. Use demo signs in as Demo. New user sits beside Use demo when `FINANCE_NEW_USER=1`, opens the same fields as setup, creates another user, and signs that user in. Without that flag, login does not offer New user and another user is refused. The owner and Demo still sign in. Setup still creates the owner when there is none. That user starts with empty accounts and bills, and with the vehicle and fuel price from that form. The owner's books and Demo's sample books stay as they were. Setup asks for a name, a password, the first vehicle, and the fallback fuel price. The vehicle has a name, a kind, and a minimum and maximum miles per gallon, and it is starred because the fuel label uses that vehicle. The price is stored on that vehicle, in cents. An empty price means miles only. The owner is the user created at setup. Demo is the sample user. Each user sees only their own accounts, bills, buckets, pay cycles, and envelopes.

The app is dark by default. The theme color is `#101714`, and `#f4efe4` with the light theme. A sidebar pill stores the light or dark theme. Each vehicle stores its own fallback fuel price. Less motion from the system still softens what plays. Teal marks checking, Needs, rent, assets, and primary actions. Sky marks savings accounts, the Savings group, electricity, water, and paid bills. Lime marks cash, groceries, debt, and a truck. Coral marks credit cards, liabilities, restaurants, and healthcare. Rose marks loans, lodging, and an RV. Plum marks Wants and subscriptions. Stone marks other accounts, other bills, paused bills, a semi, and an irregular pay cycle. Internet and cellular are iris. Fuel and travel are amber. Mortgage is blossom. Storage is sea. Propane and repairs are apricot. Maintenance is fern. Insurance is tide. Roadside and emergency are poppy. Mailbox is lemon. Laundry and liquidities are lagoon. Memberships are violet. Pets are berry. Misc and investments are citrus. A vehicle bill and a car are sage. Entertainment is blush. Shopping shares plum with subscriptions. Weekly is teal, biweekly is sky, semimonthly is plum, and monthly is lime. Amber marks amounts left out of the net-worth total. Fraunces is used for the net worth figure and for group titles. Outfit is the rest of the UI. A mark sits beside each account type, bucket group, bill type, vehicle kind, and bill status. Amount color still follows side: teal for an asset, coral for a liability.

Planner, for the browser's local date sent as `today`:

The heading is the word Planner. When a stay is underway on that date and its city is known, the heading is an italic Fraunces phrase that names the city: Welcome to {city}, Hello from {city}, Settled in {city}, or Good to be in {city}. The same stay keeps the same phrase. The stay is the open stay, the first in arrival order whose dates cover today. A shared handoff date uses that earlier stay. No underway stay, or an underway stay with no city, leaves the word Planner in the usual type. The heading is a label. It does not move a balance or change which stay is selected.

Net worth stays fixed at the top left: assets minus liabilities, in cents, with every account included. The figure is smaller than a full display size. Assets and liabilities sit on one line under it. The amount does not roll.

Six widgets fill the rest of that screen. The default order is Income beside net worth, then Calendar, Trips, and Accounts in three columns, then Envelopes as one strip, then Bills. On a phone those three columns stack. A handle on each widget drags it. The others reflow, and the order is saved on the user as `planner_order`. An unknown or partial order falls back to that default, and a widget is never dropped. A wrench on each widget sets that widget's column span on the six-column grid, saved on the user as `planner_spans`: single is 2, half is 3, and full is 6. Income defaults to half beside net worth. Calendar, Trips, and Accounts default to single. Envelopes and Bills default to full. Anything else falls back to those spans. On a phone the row still stacks into one column. New Log stays in the menu.

- Income shows the soonest payday: the payor, the date under the payor, and the amount in the display face, plus a count when there is more than one payor. It also shows Still coming in, the paychecks in the month on screen dated today or later. A payday before today has been received and is left out. It links to Income. On that date the kicker reads Payday in teal and the line settles once. Any other day the line is still
- Calendar is titled Calendar. Today's weekday and date sit in the same teal ring and pill as today on the Calendar page, and that day fills the widget even when it has no items. It links to Calendar
- Trip is titled Trip. It leads with a stay that arrives today or tomorrow, ahead of a stay that is only underway. Otherwise it shows the stay whose dates cover today: place, city, and region, with the lodging mark. The day after tomorrow does not lead. No current stay is one muted line linking to Trips. The cell opens that stay, or Trips when there is none. It does not move a balance
- Accounts is titled Accounts. It shows one column per account type that has an account: checking, savings, cash, credit, loan, fuel, and other. Each column is that type's total. Credit, loan, and fuel use coral. Empty types are skipped. It links to Accounts. Totals only
- Envelopes is titled Envelopes. Needs, Wants, and Savings are the 50/30/20 comparison, each with the same resting gauge as a locked bucket on New Log: assigned against that group's share of available to assign, filled from the bottom. There is no striped preview and no separate Assign button. Opening a row lists that group's buckets and the amounts assigned in the current month. Available to assign is one line under the strip, with the amount assigned and the pool. Available-to-assign subtracts each bucket's maximum. Planner does not step the assignment window
- Bills for the current month or the next month, as one pie of bill types, smaller than before, with its legend. Current Month and Next Month are compact choices. A slice is every unpaid occurrence of an active bill in that month. Paid and paused stay out. A type with no due amount is omitted. Each slice uses that type's color, and the legend names the type and the amount. Mark paid still does not move a balance. The wedges do not animate

`horizon_days` stays stored on the user and is unused on this screen. Calendar, at `/calendar`, is a month of stays, paydays, and bill due dates and latest paid dates. A stay is a sky box on each day from arrival through departure, with a small tree mark, the place name, and those dates. The day dialog shows that same tree mark. An unpaid occurrence is a coral box. A paid occurrence is a lilac box on the due date that was paid. A repeating bill keeps that paid date and shows the next due date as unpaid. Later months also show each date the series comes due, stepped forward from that next unpaid date. A projected date is unpaid, and a date that already holds the paid occurrence is not projected again. The box shows the name, a small mark for that bill type, and the amount as a negative. The day dialog names the bill type in text, shows that same mark, and colors the amount coral for an unpaid occurrence and lilac for a paid occurrence. A payday is a teal box with the payor, a small mark for the cycle length, and the paycheck preview as a positive. The day dialog shows that same length mark. Each day shows at most three of those boxes. A day with more shows a down arrow, and that day's list scrolls so the rest can be reached. The day number stays at the top of the day while the list scrolls. Show Income, Show Stays, Show Paid Bills, and Show Unpaid Bills sit above the grid, checked, and hide that kind on the grid only. The browser's local today is marked on that date alone. The bar under the grid shows Income in teal, Debt in coral, Net, then a rule and italic Paid in lilac. Debt is every unpaid occurrence of an active bill, as a positive amount. Weekly counts each date. Paused and archived stay off. Net is Income minus Debt. Paid is not subtracted again. A bill in another currency is an extra red total and is not added into that net. Stays do not change those totals. The bar is a label. It does not list the fuel label. Previous month and Next month stay. A day opens that day's stays, then income, then bills, including an empty section. Below the md breakpoint the month is one full-width day per row, in date order, with the weekday on the day.

Signed-out visitors see setup or login instead of the shell.

The menu stays those nine hubs. Module status is in [the module list](README.md). Themes beyond light and dark wait there too.
