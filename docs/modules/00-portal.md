# Module 0 — Portal

The signed-in app is a side menu and a main pane. Below the `md` breakpoint the menu is a drawer opened from the top bar. On a wide screen a collapse control narrows the menu to an italic N, a New log mark, the page icons, the account's initial, a round button showing the current night or day, and Log out. Pointing at one of those shows its name after a short pause. Clicking the round button switches to the other setting. The narrow menu is not used below the `md` breakpoint. The menu is New log, then Planner, Calendar, and Trips, then Income, Bills, and Budget, then Accounts and Vehicles. A light rule sits under Finance, under New log, under Trips, and under Budget. New log is larger, bold, centered, and white on the primary teal, with corners half as round as the other menu pills. The footer sits at the bottom: a light rule, the account name, Reset demo data when the signed-in user is Demo, a light rule, then a night and day pill immediately above Log out. The pill is one rounded control split into a moon half and a sun half. The active half is teal with a black icon, and the other half is black with a white icon. Anyone who is not Demo opens their name there to change that name or password. Income opens the payor list at `/pay-cycles`. Add bucket on Budget opens a new bucket. The bucket list is Budget. `/buckets` opens Budget. Planner still links to Bills. Vehicles is a direct menu link. Accounts does not link to them. A skip link moves to the main pane. Demo's menu also has Reset demo data, which asks to reset the sample books before it restores them.

Login asks for a name and a password. Use demo signs in as Demo. New user sits beside Use demo when `FINANCE_NEW_USER=1`, opens the same fields as setup, creates another user, and signs that user in. Without that flag, login does not offer New user and another user is refused. The owner and Demo still sign in. Setup still creates the owner when there is none. That user starts with empty accounts and bills, and with the vehicle and fuel price from that form. The owner's books and Demo's sample books stay as they were. Setup asks for a name, a password, the first vehicle, and the fallback fuel price. The vehicle has a name, a kind, and a minimum and maximum miles per gallon, and it is starred because the fuel label uses that vehicle. The price is stored on that vehicle, in cents. An empty price means miles only. The owner is the user created at setup. Demo is the sample user. Each user sees only their own accounts, bills, buckets, pay cycles, budget, and calendar entries.

The app is dark by default. The theme color is `#101714`, and `#f4efe4` with the light theme. A sidebar pill stores the light or dark theme. Each vehicle stores its own fallback fuel price. Less motion from the system still softens what plays. Teal marks checking, Needs, rent, assets, and primary actions. Sky marks savings accounts, the Savings group, electricity, water, and paid bills. Lime marks cash, groceries, debt, and a truck. Coral marks credit cards, liabilities, restaurants, and healthcare. Rose marks loans, lodging, and an RV. Plum marks Wants and subscriptions. Stone marks other accounts, other bills, paused bills, a semi, and an irregular pay cycle. Internet and cellular are iris. Fuel, travel, and a utv are amber. Mortgage is blossom. Storage is sea. Propane and repairs are apricot. Maintenance and an atv are fern. Insurance is tide. Roadside and emergency are poppy. Mailbox and a golf cart are lemon. Laundry and liquidities are lagoon. Memberships are violet. Pets and a dirt bike are berry. Misc and investments are citrus. A vehicle bill and a car are sage. Entertainment is blush. Shopping shares plum with subscriptions. Weekly is teal, biweekly is sky, semimonthly is plum, and monthly is lime. Amber marks amounts left out of the net-worth total. Fraunces is used for the net worth figure and for group titles. Outfit is the rest of the UI. A mark sits beside each account type, bucket group, bill type, vehicle kind, and bill status. Amount color still follows side: teal for an asset, coral for a liability.

Planner, for the browser's local date sent as `today`:

The heading is the word Planner. When a stay is underway on that date and its city is known, the heading is an italic Fraunces phrase that names the city. Fifty phrases share that job. Each time the planner loads, one of them is chosen at random, and that visit keeps it. The stay is the open stay, the first in arrival order whose dates cover today. A shared handoff date uses that earlier stay. No underway stay, or an underway stay with no city, leaves the word Planner in the usual type. The heading is a label. It does not move a balance or change which stay is selected.

Net worth stays fixed at the top left: assets minus liabilities, in cents, with every account included. The figure is smaller than a full display size. Assets and liabilities sit on one line under it. The amount does not roll. Interest lost sits at the lower left in coral, and interest gained sits at the lower right in fern, each as the amount followed by /mn.

Six widgets fill the rest of that screen. The default order is Income beside net worth, then Calendar, Trips, and Accounts in three columns, then Budget as one strip, then Bills. On a phone those three columns stack. A handle on each widget drags it on a wide screen. On a phone that handle is hidden. The wrench opens Edit {name} layout, with Move back and Move forward. Move back swaps that widget one place earlier in `planner_order` (left on a wide screen, up when the widgets stack). Move forward swaps it one place later (right, or down when stacked). The first widget cannot move back. The last cannot move forward. The others reflow, and the order is saved on the user as `planner_order`. An unknown or partial order falls back to that default, and a widget is never dropped. That same wrench sets the widget's column span on the six-column grid, saved on the user as `planner_spans`. The control is a slider from 1 to 6. On a phone the slider is locked, and the current span still shows. Income defaults to 3 beside net worth. Calendar, Trips, and Accounts default to 2. Budget and Bills default to 6. Anything else falls back to those spans. On a phone the row still stacks into one column. New log stays in the menu.

- Income shows the soonest payday: the payor, the date under the payor, and the amount in the display face, plus a count when there is more than one payor. It also shows Still coming in, the paychecks in the current month dated today or later. The Bills month choice does not change that figure. A payday before today has been received and is left out. It links to Income. On that date the kicker reads Payday in teal and the line settles once. Any other day the line is still
- Calendar is titled Calendar. Span 1 or 2 shows one day, today. Span 3 or 4 shows two days. Span 5 or 6 shows three days. Those days are today and the following days, in calendar order, in one row that shares the widget width. Each day is the same square as that date on the Calendar page: the date pill, then that day's stays, calendar entries, paydays, and bills. Clicking a calendar entry opens it. Today keeps the teal ring. Three items stay in view, and the rest scroll. A day with no items still shows the date. Each day links to Calendar. On a phone the widget stacks to one column, and the day count still follows the span
- Trip is titled Trip. It leads with a stay that arrives today or tomorrow, ahead of a stay that is only underway. Otherwise it shows the stay whose dates cover today: place, city, and region, with the lodging mark. The day after tomorrow does not lead. No current stay is one muted line linking to Trips. The cell opens that stay, or Trips when there is none. It does not move a balance
- Accounts is titled Accounts. It shows one column per account type that has an account: checking, savings, cash, credit, loan, fuel, and other. Each column is that type's total. Credit, loan, and fuel use coral. Empty types are skipped. It links to Accounts. Totals only
- Budget is titled Budget. Needs, Wants, and Savings are the 50/30/20 comparison, each with the same resting gauge as a locked bucket on New Log: assigned against that group's share of the income still coming in plus those accounts, filled from the bottom. There is no striped preview and no separate Assign button. Opening a row lists that group's buckets and the amounts assigned in the current month. Available to assign is one line under the strip: income still coming in this month, plus every asset marked Include in Budget, minus what the buckets already hold. The amount under it is what is already assigned of that income plus those accounts. Planner does not step the assignment window
- Bills for the current month or the next month, as one pie of bill types, smaller than before, with its legend. Current Month and Next Month are compact choices. A slice is every unpaid occurrence of an active bill in that month. Paid and paused stay out. A type with no due amount is omitted. Each slice uses that type's color, and the legend names the type and the amount. Mark paid still does not move a balance. The wedges do not animate

The period in the sidebar sits between the previous and next arrows. Its start and length are saved from the name dialog. Calendar, at `/calendar`, draws the days inside that period: stays, paydays, and bill due dates and latest paid dates. Empty cells only line the first day up under its weekday. A stay is a sky box on each day from arrival through departure, with a small tree mark, the place name, and those dates. The day dialog shows that same tree mark. An unpaid occurrence is a coral box. A paid occurrence is a lilac box on the due date that was paid. A repeating bill keeps that paid date and shows the next due date as unpaid. Later months also show each date the series comes due, stepped forward from that next unpaid date. A projected date is unpaid, and a date that already holds the paid occurrence is not projected again. The box shows the name, a small mark for that bill type, and the amount as a negative. The day dialog names the bill type in text, shows that same mark, and colors the amount coral for an unpaid occurrence and lilac for a paid occurrence. A payday is a teal box with the payor, a small mark for the cycle length, and the paycheck preview as a positive. The day dialog shows that same length mark. A calendar entry is a stone box on each day from its start through its end, with its text. Add entry, at the top right, opens that start date, end date, and text. The end is on or after the start. Clicking the box edits the text and the dates, or deletes the entry. Entries stay on the grid when income, stays, or bills are hidden, and they do not change the month totals. Each day shows at most three of those boxes. A day with more shows a down arrow, and that day's list scrolls so the rest can be reached. The day number stays at the top of the day while the list scrolls. Show Income, Show Stays, Show Paid Bills, and Show Unpaid Bills sit above the grid, checked, and hide that kind on the grid only. The browser's local today is marked on that date alone. The bar under the grid shows Income in teal, Debt in coral, Net, then a rule and italic Paid in lilac. Debt is every unpaid occurrence of an active bill, as a positive amount. Weekly counts each date. Paused and archived stay off. Net is Income minus Debt. Paid is not subtracted again. A bill in another currency is an extra red total and is not added into that net. Stays do not change those totals. The bar is a label. It does not list the fuel label. Previous month and Next month stay. A day opens that day's stays, then income, then bills, including an empty section. Below the md breakpoint the month is one full-width day per row, in date order, with the weekday on the day.
