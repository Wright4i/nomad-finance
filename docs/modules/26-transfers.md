# Module 26 — Transfers

Status: now

A transfer moves two balances. Posting stays retired. A bill still does not move a balance by itself. See [a transfer moves balances](../adr/0027-a-transfer-moves-balances.md).

Pay a paid-in-full card. The card list shows each open paid-in-full card that has a balance, and hides a card at $0.00. The label is the account label, then the balance, as in Camp card - Camp bank (Ada) - $20.00. The screen order is that card, then the account the cash comes from beside the balance not covered by buckets, then the bill buckets, then Total payment. The pay-from account card is half the row, the same width as the card-balance card, and the other half is the balance not covered by the selected bills. That amount is Total payment minus those bills. It cannot be typed. Clicking it adds that remainder to the card's water. Review stays off until a bill bucket is selected or Not covered by buckets is pressed. The pay-from list is the open asset accounts, including one at $0.00. The card shows as an account card, balance first. Total payment is the full payment. It carries a dollar mark and starts at the card's balance. Choosing a bucket does not change it. That amount is the cash that leaves the pay-from account and the cash that lowers the card. A bucket on this screen is an unpaid bill drawn from the card that is due today or older. A bill due today is on the list. A bill due after today is not. A paid bill, a paused bill, an archived bill, and a bill drawn from another account stay off the list. Clicking a bucket selects that bill's full amount. The selected bills cannot add up to more than the payment, and a bill that would pass it stays off. The card is a bucket of water. The water is the selected bill buckets filling toward the payment, plus the uncovered balance while that card is pressed. The water grows up from the bottom in 500ms, including from empty. It is red while those buckets are short of the payment, and green when they reach it. The part of the payment past the buckets is still cash from the pay-from account. Confirm is a modal. It draws the full payment from the pay-from account to the card in the account type colors, with the balance each account will have after. A bar splits that payment into the buckets and the cash beyond the buckets. The buckets are marked paid and those buckets drop, and that drop does not move more cash. When the buckets equal the payment, available to assign stays the same. The cash beyond the buckets is still the same payment. It is not a second move. The modal names the bills that will be marked paid. The confirm modal says the payment may be posted to the card from that account after Confirm. Typing Total payment does not leave that note on the screen. Nothing is marked paid and neither balance moves until that modal is confirmed. Paid and Transferred then show as a celebration over the screen. When the Paid celebration finishes, that same note opens again as a modal, and Ok closes it. On confirm, the pay-from account and the card each decrease by the full payment, and each selected bill is marked paid and its bucket is lowered by that bill. Neither balance goes below zero. The payment cannot be more than the card's balance or the pay-from account's balance. Paying a card can be a partial payment of the balance.

Transfer between asset accounts. The From list is open asset accounts that have a balance, and it hides an account at $0.00. The To list is every open asset account, including one at $0.00. Both labels are the account label, then the balance, as in Everyday checking - $3,180.00 or Empty jar - $0.00. Choosing an account shows that account the way the Accounts list does. The amount carries a dollar mark. Confirm is a modal. It draws the source, the destination, and the amount in the account type colors. Balances stay put until that modal is confirmed. The amount is at least one cent and no more than the From account's balance. Confirm is available when that balance covers the amount. The only Short is the dollars asked for beyond what that account holds. Unassigned cash, a bucket, a goal bucket, and leaving Include in Budget do not block Confirm and do not show a Short. An in-budget asset moved to another in-budget asset changes the two balances and leaves available-to-assign and the buckets alone. A transfer that leaves Include in Budget takes unassigned cash first, then money from buckets. A locked bucket is not pulled below its bills. The pull does not wait to be accepted. When the buckets do not cover the rest, the two balances still move.
