# Module 19 — Tracking accounts

Status: later

A tracking account counts in net worth and stays out of available-to-assign. Its value is a number you type. Use it for a house, a vehicle, or cash you do not spend from. The word is tracking account.

It uses the account fields that already exist, plus a tracking flag. Side still says whether it adds to or subtracts from net worth.

Demo seed: one tracking asset named Tracking 1, with a typed value, present on the dashboard and absent from the assignment pool.

Requires accounts. The pool rule is enforced when envelopes exist. Before envelopes, the flag still keeps the account in net worth.
