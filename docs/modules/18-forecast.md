# Module 18 — Forecast

Status: later

Before postings exist, the forecast places the bills you already have onto the pay periods their due dates fall in. It does not project a cash balance. A projected balance waits for postings.

Demo seed: Phone shown on the period that contains its next due date.

Requires bills. Pay periods come from pay cycles once that module exists. Until then the forecast can list bills by due date only, and the period placement arrives with pay cycles.
