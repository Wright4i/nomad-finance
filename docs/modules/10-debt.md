# Module 10 — Credit picture

Status: dropped

The Credit screen, payoff math, and utilization are removed. The account fields planned payment, horizon, balance mark, and original amount are removed. The horizon that remains is the calendar window on the user. Annual interest, minimum due, due day, and credit limit stay on the account. A card over its limit still warns on confirm, per [a confirmed posting moves the stored balance](../adr/0016-posting-moves-balance.md). The gauges stay on the calendar.

The demo seed no longer stores those four fields.
