# Module 17 — Age of money

Status: blocked

Age of money is the number of days between a dollar arriving and that dollar leaving. Typed balances have no such history. This module waits until postings exist, and the posting ADR has decided what a posting means for the balance.

Demo seed, when it is unblocked: one inflow and one later outflow far enough apart to show a day count.

Blocked on [postings](11-postings.md).
