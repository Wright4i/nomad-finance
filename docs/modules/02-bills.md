# Module 2 — Bills

A bill is a reminder drawn from one account. The list shows one status at a time. Active, Paused, Paid, and Archived are tabs across the top. A filter narrows the open tab by name, draw-from account, bill type, and amount, and that filter applies before the day window. A locked bucket's name opens this list with that bill type already chosen. The filter stays in the address. Back returns to that filter. Bills in the sidebar opens the list with the filter cleared. The date is a label, a horizontal line sits under that label, and that date's bills sit two across. The open tab has its own scrollbar, and that list fills the screen under the filter. The list shows bills with an occurrence in the period. Overdue unpaid bills still appear when the period contains today, counted once. Active, paused, and archived open on the earliest dates and load later dates when the scroll reaches the end. Paid opens on the most recent dates and loads older dates when the scroll reaches the end. Dates within what is shown run from oldest to newest. A closed row shows the icon, the name, the type, and the amount. The arrow opens chips for the draw-from account, the bucket, autopay, and recurrence, and the actions sit with those chips.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Required, trimmed, 1–80 characters |
| Type | A needs, wants, or savings kind. Fuel homes on the locked Fuel bucket. Other and misc each store a name you type |
| Draw-from account | Required, not archived. A new bill starts on the starred account. A locked bill draws from the starred account. With none starred, a new bill starts on the first open account and a locked bill draws from the account that keeps it |
| Amount | Integer cents, greater than zero |
| Recurrence | once, weekly, monthly, yearly |
| Every | 1 to 11. Shown under Recurrence when the bill repeats. Weekly counts weeks, monthly counts months, yearly counts years. Once stores 1 and hides the slider |
| Next due date | Calendar date. Becomes the anchor when you save the schedule |
| Autopay | Flag only. A new bill starts checked |
| Notes | Optional, up to 2000 characters |

Mark paid records that occurrence on the due date it had. A repeating bill then advances by its every from that due date. Monthly and yearly keep the anchor day and clamp to the last day of a short month, so 31 January becomes 28 February and then 31 March. The new date can still sit in the past. A one-off becomes paid and leaves the due list. The paid occurrence of a repeating bill stays on that due date, counts in the paid total, and offers Unpay only. The next occurrence stays unpaid and offers Mark paid only. One occurrence does not show both. Unpay removes that latest paid occurrence and leaves the bill in place. A repeating bill moves its next due date back by its every. A one-off becomes active again. Another unpay is refused, because only that latest date is stored. Pause hides a bill from due-soon and leaves it on the list. Resume puts it back on due-soon. Mark paid is for active bills. A paid one-off does not block archiving the draw-from account. An active or paused bill does. Autopay stays a flag. Mark paid and unpay leave the balance as you last typed it. A fuel bill can store a vehicle, an odometer, and a price per gallon. Those readings do not change the account balance. New Log stores that fuel bill, and a need, want, or saving log, unpaid. A need log offers every needs type except Fuel. Mark paid comes later, the same way as any other bill, and it leaves the account balance alone. An unpaid fill-up still shows on the vehicle and still feeds the current odometer, the newest price, and miles per gallon. New Log for Fuel opens the odometer on that vehicle's current odometer and the price per gallon on the newest priced fill-up for that vehicle, by the latest fill-up date. On the same date, the later log wins. With none logged, it uses that vehicle's fallback price and otherwise leaves the price blank. Another vehicle's price is not used. An edit to the odometer or the price stays when the vehicle changes. Miles traveled is the typed reading minus the current odometer. Gallons are the amount divided by the price per gallon. Miles per gallon is those miles divided by those gallons, shown to one decimal before the log is saved, once the price and the amount are entered and the miles are known. The fuel log shows that distance as a teal square, the figure and "mi." over the word traveled. When miles per gallon can be calculated, a red square sits beside it, the figure over mpg. The type is the kind of reminder, and it picks the group. Other and misc each ask for a name you type, from 1 to 80 characters, and the list shows that name. A preset type stores no extra name. Every bill sits on a bucket in that group. A fuel bill sits on Fuel Bucket and cannot move. Saving, moving, pausing, or resuming the bill writes its unpaid dues into that bucket on the oldest active pay cycle. Mark paid and unpay leave the assignment alone. See [a bill fills its bucket](../adr/0020-bill-fills-bucket.md).

Calendar shows a repeating bill's paid occurrence on the due date that was paid, and the next occurrence on its new due date. Later months show each following due date as unpaid, stepped forward from that next date by the bill's recurrence and every. A paid occurrence is not also drawn as unpaid. An unpaid occurrence is a coral box and a paid occurrence is a lilac box, with the name, a small mark for that bill type, and the amount. The day dialog shows that same mark and colors the amount the same way. The month bar's Paid figure includes those paid amounts. Stays and paydays share that month. The month on screen labels the sum of the amounts shown. A note when you save a higher amount than the one already stored still waits. That price note needs no posting. [Forecast](18-forecast.md) places these due dates onto pay periods.
