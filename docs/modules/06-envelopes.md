# Module 6 — Budget

Status: now

Assign cash into buckets for the month across every payor. This module follows [typed balances](../adr/0001-typed-balances.md). Envelope is the amount you type. Bill assignments and Target are locked. Neither replaces the account balance.

Available to assign is income still coming in this month, plus every asset marked Include in Budget, minus what the buckets already hold. A payday before today is left out. The line under it is what is already assigned of that income plus those accounts. Needs is 50% of that income plus those accounts, Wants 30%, and Savings 20%, half up to the cent. The modal lists each paycheck still coming in on its own line with its date, then those accounts. A section with no rows is left off. The lines add up to the amount under the headline. A transfer between those accounts leaves that portion unchanged. Credit cards, loans, fuel accounts, and tracking accounts stay out. A goal's amount to hold stays on the bucket and does not reduce the total. A locked bucket's unpaid bills are counted once for the month, not once per payor. Upcoming and closed months do not change the number.

An assignment is zero or more. See [rollover](07-rollover.md).

You assign to the period. The period that contains today is editable, and available-to-assign follows it. A period that ends before today is closed and view only. Its Available to assign card is hidden, the page says this period has closed, and every envelope input is disabled. A period that starts after today can be planned, and available does not follow it. A payday counts as income only when that date falls inside the accounting period. Every such payday counts. The pay period around it does not hold an assignment and does not change available to assign. The envelope is one amount for the bucket. Typing it on the period on screen, including a later period, changes it on every period. Bills due in the period are auto-assigned, and that is the only amount that differs between periods. A goal, a goal with no target, and a locked bucket follow the same rule. Fuel still includes trip fuel for stays in the period. Uncovered float on a paid-in-full credit or fuel card is subtracted from available. That amount is the card balance minus the unpaid bills drawn from the card that are already in the buckets. A $2,200 card with $2,000 of those bills takes $200. The available list shows that card only when the uncovered amount is not zero, and the line is that amount as a negative number.

Each group heading names Needs, Wants, or Savings and, beside that name, Auto-assigned, Envelope, and Target for the accounting period on screen, then that group's goal in parentheses. Auto-assigned is the sum of the auto-assigned amounts. Envelope is the sum of the envelopes. Target is the sum of the targets. The parenthetical is Needs 50%, Wants 30%, or Savings 20% of income still coming in plus the assets marked Include in Budget, half up to the cent, written as (50% goal - $1,234.56). That parenthetical turns red when the group's target is over the goal. The planner gauges keep their share of goal assignments.

Each bucket is a tile: name, glyph, and three fields. Bill assignments is a chip above its amount, then Envelope and Target. Goal tiles come first. Locked tiles follow, with a lock. Bill assignments and Target are locked. Envelope is the only field you type. Target is the bill assignments plus the envelope, and it follows the envelope as you type. A bucket with a target balance hides the Bill assignments chip and its number. That place holds a Goal based chip in the same space, so Envelope and Target line up with the other tiles, and Target shows the amount to hold. A goal with no target hides the Bill assignments chip and Target. That place holds a No goal chip, and Envelope stays on the same line. A closed period can be read. The water rises from the bottom in 500ms, including from empty, and the surface turns. A target balance fills by the envelope divided by the amount to hold. Above that amount the water stays full, turns orange, and a red footer under the tile reads Over by the difference. A locked bucket with money fills by the group's targets divided by its 50%, 30%, or 20% goal, so every funded locked bucket in that group shows the same level. An empty locked bucket stays empty. The water reaches full as the group reaches the goal, and it turns orange only when the group's targets pass the goal. The amount past the goal is split evenly across the locked buckets that hold money, half up to the cent, and each of those tiles shows that share in a red Over by footer. A manual bucket with no target maps $0 through $1,000 onto empty through full, and it does not turn orange. A needed-by-date goal still fills by the target divided by the amount needed by that date. Zero is empty. Envelope saves one second after the last change, and Enter saves the same way. Recalculate asks first, then sets Bill assignments to the bills in the period on screen. The question names that period the way the pager does, such as Oct 16 – Nov 15. Fuel also includes each stay that arrives in that period and has not ended. Envelope amounts stay as typed. A closed period cannot be recalculated. A log is an unpaid one-time bill. It raises Bill assignments. The envelope stays as typed.

A locked bucket's target on screen starts at the bills in that month, counted once, and the envelope can raise it. The Fuel bucket also counts each stay that arrives in that month and has not ended, once, by that stay's fuel estimate. A stay that has ended is left out. A stay that only continues from an earlier arrival does not count again. A flight, a stay with no driving miles, and a stay with no price add nothing. The account balance stays as typed. An overdue unpaid date counts in the open month once. Available to assign follows the open month across every payor. Paying a bill takes that occurrence out of Bill assignments. The envelope stays as typed. A goal envelope stays as typed and lowers available to assign by that amount. It does not change an account balance. Pause takes the bill out. Resume puts it back. The row lists each contributing bill and its amount. A needed-by-date tile does not show the date. A target balance tile shows the amount to hold in Target.

Reaching zero available-to-assign celebrates once in that month.

Demo seed: Road cushion has one envelope, shown on the closed month and the open month. Camp jar and Glovebox are in the pool. Rig fund stays out of the pool and stays in net worth.

Each payor has an open period, and that period only says when the paycheck arrives. Available to assign is one total for the books. The envelope is one amount per bucket, not one amount per period. See [one cash pool](../adr/0012-one-pool-across-payors.md).
