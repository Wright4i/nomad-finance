# Module 6 — Budget

Status: now

Assign cash into buckets for the month across every payor. This module follows [typed balances](../adr/0001-typed-balances.md). Envelope is the amount you type. Auto-assigned and Target are locked. Neither replaces the account balance.

Available to assign is income still coming in this month, plus every asset marked Include in Budget, minus what the buckets already hold. A payday before today is left out. The line under it is what is already assigned of that income plus those accounts. Needs is 50% of that income plus those accounts, Wants 30%, and Savings 20%, half up to the cent. The modal lists the paychecks still coming in and those accounts, and the lines add up to the amount under the headline. A transfer between those accounts leaves that portion unchanged. Credit cards, loans, fuel accounts, and tracking accounts stay out. A goal's amount to hold stays on the bucket and does not reduce the total. A locked bucket's unpaid bills are counted once for the month, not once per payor. Upcoming and closed months do not change the number.

An assignment is zero or more. See [rollover](07-rollover.md).

You assign in the current month, and you can plan the next one. Current Month and Next Month move the assignment window. Previous month opens a closed month. Amounts you type for the next month are in place when it opens. Until then, available-to-assign still follows the current month. A month you have not planned opens empty, except the bills due that month. Each month keeps the amount assigned to each bucket after it closes. A closed month can be read and cannot be edited. A correction belongs in the current month.

Each group heading names Needs, Wants, or Savings and, beside that name, Auto-assigned, Envelope, and Target for the month on screen, then that group's goal in parentheses. Auto-assigned is the sum of the auto-assigned amounts. Envelope is the sum of the envelopes. Target is the sum of the targets. The parenthetical is Needs 50%, Wants 30%, or Savings 20% of income still coming in plus the assets marked Include in Budget, half up to the cent, written as (50% goal - $1,234.56). That parenthetical turns red when the group's target is over the goal. The planner gauges keep their share of goal assignments.

Each bucket is a tile: name, glyph, and three fields, Auto-assigned, Envelope, and Target. Goal tiles come first. Locked tiles follow, with a lock. Auto-assigned and Target are locked. Envelope is the only field you type. Target is auto-assigned plus the envelope, and it follows the envelope as you type. A closed month can be read. The water rises from the bottom, and the surface turns. When an amount to hold is saved and above zero the fill is the target divided by that amount; above it the water stays full, turns orange, and a red footer under the tile reads Over by the difference. With no amount to hold the fill is this target divided by the largest in the group. Zero is empty. Envelope saves one second after the last change, and Enter saves the same way. Recalculate asks first, then replaces every assignment for the month on screen with the bills that occur in that month. Fuel also includes each stay that arrives in that month and has not ended. Amounts you typed are replaced, so the envelope returns to zero. A closed month cannot be recalculated. A log is an unpaid one-time bill. It raises auto-assigned, and the envelope shrinks by the same amount when it has room.

A locked bucket's target on screen starts at the bills in that month, counted once, and the envelope can raise it. The Fuel bucket also counts each stay that arrives in that month and has not ended, once, by that stay's fuel estimate. A stay that has ended is left out. A stay that only continues from an earlier arrival does not count again. A flight, a stay with no driving miles, and a stay with no price add nothing. The account balance stays as typed. An overdue unpaid date counts in the open month once. Available to assign follows the open month across every payor. Paying a bill lowers that locked assignment, and it lowers the locked maximum when no amount to hold is saved. A goal assignment stays as typed and lowers available to assign by that amount. It does not change an account balance. Pause takes the bill out. Resume puts it back. The row lists each contributing bill and its amount. A tile does not show a date or a still-to-hold line.

Reaching zero available-to-assign celebrates once in that month.

Demo seed: a current month with one assignment on Road cushion, and a closed month that still has its row. Camp jar and Glovebox are in the pool. Rig fund stays out of the pool and stays in net worth.

Each payor has an open span. Available to assign is one total for the books, and an assignment is stored on one payor's span. See [one cash pool](../adr/0012-one-pool-across-payors.md). The pay-period tables keep their names.
