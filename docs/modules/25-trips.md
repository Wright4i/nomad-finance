# Module 25 — Trips

Status: now

A trip is one place: a name, an optional note, an arrival, a departure, and one pin. A second park is a second trip. Arrival and departure both count as days of the stay. The calendar shows the place on each of those days.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Place name | Required, trimmed, 1–80 characters. The only name |
| Note | Optional, up to 2000 characters. Shown with the name, such as a site number |
| Rating | Optional. An integer count of half-stars. The screen offers 1 through 10, a half star through 5, and shows that number with one decimal beside a star, such as 1.0 or 1.5. Empty means unrated. Saving it does not change a balance |
| Comment | Optional, trimmed, up to 2000 characters. Shown after a rating is set, on the same row as the stars on a wide screen and under them on a narrow one. It saves about a second after typing stops. An empty comment is stored as none. Refused when the stay is unrated |
| Arrival, departure | Calendar dates. Departure is on or after arrival |
| Coordinates | Required. Search, a dragged pin, or typed latitude and longitude |
| Draw-from account | Required when the total is greater than zero. One account for the deposit and the amount due |
| Total | Integer cents, zero or greater |
| Amount due | Integer cents, zero or greater, and not above the total |
| Balance-due date | Optional. Prefilled with arrival. Saving it does not change a balance |

The deposit is total minus amount due. It is not stored. Saving a trip does not move a balance. When the amount due is greater than zero and the stay has a balance-due date, the stay keeps one lodging bill: the place name, the amount due, the stay account and that account's currency, due once on the balance-due date. Editing the place, the amount due, the date, or the account updates that same bill. Clearing the amount due or the date removes it. Mark paid is the ordinary bill action and does not change the account balance. Deleting a trip removes that bill and does not change an account balance. An active lodging bill blocks archiving its account, the same as any active bill.

Two stays may share a handoff date and may not share a night. Overlap is one arriving before the other departs, and the other arriving before the first departs. Create and update both refuse an overlap and name the other stay.

The screen opens on a 3D globe with the month under it. Stays take colors in arrival order: poppy, lagoon, berry, citrus, violet, apricot, tide, blossom, fern, lilac, lemon, sea, then those twelve again, so two stays next to each other do not share a color. A day between arrival and departure is filled with that color. Arrival is the upper-right half of a split from the top-left corner to the bottom-right. Departure is the lower-left half. A shared handoff date shows both. Clicking that day selects the stay that is arriving. The open stay is a card in its color. The card opens the stay editor. That card shows a sky balance-due chip when the stay has a balance-due date: the scale mark, the amount due in money, and the date as a short month and day, such as $120.00 Due Nov 22. A zero amount still shows as $0.00. The chip stays hidden when the stay has no balance-due date. It shows an amber fuel-amount chip, labeled Fuel and the cost, when the stay has a fuel cost, a violet miles-per-gallon chip when the stay has miles per gallon, and a coral price-per-gallon chip when that cost has a price, such as $4.00/gal. Each chip has its own mark. A missing figure hides its chip. It shows a fern miles chip, such as 270 miles, when the stay has driving miles from the previous stay, and hides that chip when it does not. Each stay in that month's list uses its color. Locate on that stay flies the globe to its pin and zooms in to street level, including when that stay is already centered. The pencil opens the editor. The row itself does not move the globe. Night uses Positron and Day uses Voyager. A control on the globe chooses how many past months to draw, from 0 through 12, and it starts at 0. Zero leaves out past months, and current and future stays stay drawn. Editing a stay uses satellite imagery, zoomed in on the pin. That pin is labeled with the stay name and the street address. Each pin is labeled with the place name and the city and state. The screen opens on today's month and the stay that covers today. When none covers today, it opens the soonest stay that has not ended. Previous steps to the next older stay and does not wrap. Next only goes forward. Every leg asks the driving router once, and again if that request fails. Pins are joined by that driving line, labeled in miles. A leg with no road route is a dashed short path with a teal airplane at its middle and no miles. The path splits at the date line so the line does not wrap the long way around the map. The screen says the road route is unavailable. Miles on the leg and in the month list are the driving miles. The month list says how many miles it is from the previous stay. The open stay card shows those miles as a chip and does not repeat them in a sentence. Next and previous fly the globe to the pin in 400ms and show that stay's arrival month on the calendar. A stay already in the visible month leaves the month as it is. The rating for the open stay sits on that same row as Previous and Next. Stars are teal. An empty star is an outline, a chosen star is filled, and a half star fills the left half. Hovering a half ghosts every star from the first through that value at half opacity, a full star for each whole step and only the left half on a half step. The star under the pointer grows slightly and gains a lighter teal glow. Stars past that value stay outlines. After a rating is set, the number shows with one decimal beside a star, from 0.5 through 5.0, and the comment field sits on the same row. On a narrow screen the comment wraps under that row. The comment saves about a second after typing stops. The place name and the note show with that pin. Reduced motion moves the globe without the flight. Status is upcoming, underway, or done from the dates and today.

The fuel label uses the driving miles from the previous stay and the starred vehicle only. Miles per gallon are the total miles of that vehicle's in-range fill-ups divided by the total gallons of those same fill-ups. A fill-up is every fuel bill on that vehicle with the same odometer. An unpaid fill-up counts the same as a paid one. A fill-up with no miles per gallon is ignored. A fill-up outside the range stays on the fuel list and is left out of the average. When the starred vehicle has no in-range fill-up yet, the label uses the midpoint of its minimum and maximum. The price is the starred vehicle's newest priced fill-up, or that vehicle's fallback price when none is logged, in cents. The open stay shows the miles per gallon and that price, such as $4.00/gal, as chips beside the cost. It does not show a fuel sentence. Cost is the miles divided by that figure, times the price, half up. No starred vehicle, no previous stay, or an empty price means miles only and no cost. The first stay has no cost. The label assigns nothing and does not move a balance. The vehicle page lists one row per fill-up. See [a vehicle is its own odometer](../adr/0019-vehicle-odometer.md).

Demo seed: campground stays from Devils Garden on 2026-09-06 through La Posa South on 2027-01-08. Each stay leaves the morning the next arrives, so every day in that span is on a site, including 2026-11-05 at Usery Mountain. Lost Dutchman is unrated, with the balance due on 2026-11-22. Cottonwood stores 7 half-stars and a comment. A stay does not change an account balance.

Requires accounts. Lodging does not write a balance. See [a balance is only what you type](../adr/0022-balance-is-only-what-you-type.md).
