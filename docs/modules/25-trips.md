# Module 25 — Trips

Status: now

A trip is one place: a name, an optional note, an arrival, a departure, and one pin. A second park is a second trip. Arrival and departure both count as days of the stay. The calendar shows the place on each of those days.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Place name | Required, trimmed, 1–80 characters. The only name |
| Note | Optional, up to 2000 characters. Shown with the name, such as a site number |
| Rating | Optional. An integer count of half-stars. The screen offers 1 through 10, a half star through 5, and shows that number with one decimal beside a star, such as 1.0 or 1.5. Empty means unrated. Saving it does not change a balance |
| Comment | Optional, trimmed, up to 2000 characters. Shown after a rating is set, on the same row as the stars on a wide screen and under them on a narrow one. It saves about a second after typing stops. An empty comment is stored as none. Refused when the stay is unrated |
| Arrival, departure | Calendar dates. Departure is on or after arrival. A new stay sets departure to the day after arrival whenever arrival changes |
| Travel | Drive or fly. Fly draws a dashed line and does not ask for a road route. Drive asks for the road route and stays a dashed line with no airplane when that route is missing |
| Coordinates | Required. Search, a dragged pin, or typed latitude and longitude |
| Draw-from account | Required when the total is greater than zero. One account for the deposit and the amount due. A new stay starts on the shopping favorite, or on the first open account when none is set |
| Total | Integer cents, zero or greater |
| Amount due | Integer cents, zero or greater, and not above the total. The form copies the total into this field when the total changes |
| Balance-due date | Optional. Prefilled with arrival. Saving it does not change a balance |

The deposit is total minus amount due. It is not stored. Saving a trip does not move a balance. When the deposit is greater than zero, the stay keeps one lodging bill on the locked Lodging bucket for the deposit: the place name, the deposit still due, the stay account and that account's currency, due once on arrival. When the amount due is greater than zero and the stay has a balance-due date, the stay keeps a second lodging bill for the amount due on the balance-due date. Editing the place, the split, the date, or the account updates those bills. Clearing a side removes its bill when that bill is still unpaid. A paid lodging bill stays on the list. Mark paid is the ordinary bill action and does not change the account balance; marking the deposit bill paid lowers the deposit due on the stay, and marking the amount-due bill paid clears the amount due on the stay, including when pay a card marks it paid. Deleting a trip removes those bills and does not change an account balance. An active lodging bill blocks archiving its account, the same as any active bill.

A new stay takes a place on the route from its arrival and departure dates, the same order as the month list. Changing either date on save moves it to that place again. Earlier and Later still trade only with the stay beside it on the route. Add a stay opens with arrival on the first day of the month shown on the trip calendar.

Two stays may share a night. A stay that arrives while another is still there is kept. A stay that ends after the one it overlaps comes second on the route, because that departure is later. A stay that also ends during the other one can trade places with the stay beside it. Earlier and Later do that trade, and they do not move a stay past a stay on another day. A handoff and a stay that shares no night have no Earlier or Later. The driving line and the fuel miles follow the route. See [a shared night keeps its route](../adr/0026-shared-night-keeps-its-route.md).

The screen opens on a 3D globe at the world zoom, centered among the stays drawn on it. Past months and Forward months choose how many calendar months before and after the month on the trip calendar still draw on the globe. A stay draws when it touches that window. Forward months defaults to 12 when Trips opens from the sidebar. Past months defaults to 0. Hide names, Hide locations, and Hide miles each drop that part of the globe pins or road mile tags; flight airplane marks stay. Those choices stay with the month controls until sidebar Trips opens again. Previous month and Next month move the stay calendar, and the heading names that month, such as Nov 2026. Stepping to a stay opens the month it arrives. Sunday through Saturday stay as the headers. Empty cells only line a midweek start under its weekday. The stay-to-stay control stays. Stays take colors in arrival order: poppy, lagoon, berry, citrus, violet, apricot, tide, blossom, fern, lilac, lemon, sea, then those twelve again, so two stays next to each other do not share a color. A day between arrival and departure is filled with that color. A day inside overlapping stays fills with the nested stay, the one with the latest arrival still there. Arrival is the upper-right half of a split from the top-left corner to the bottom-right. Departure is the lower-left half. A shared handoff date shows both. A stay that arrives while another is still there shows that night the same way: the arrival on the upper-right, the stay already there on the lower-left. More than one arrival on a day shows the first, and the month list has the others. Clicking that day selects the stay that is arriving. The open stay is a card in its color. The card opens the stay editor. That card shows a balance-due chip when the amount due is greater than zero and the stay has a balance-due date: the scale mark, the amount due in money, and the date as a short month and day, such as -$120.00 Due Nov 22. Under Lodging, the total, the deposit, and the amount due each show a minus in coral. Zero stays grey, without a minus. Miles, miles per gallon, and dates stay unsigned. The price per gallon keeps the amount's color. The chip stays hidden when the amount due is zero or the stay has no balance-due date. It shows a fuel-amount chip, labeled Fuel and the cost, when the fuel cost is greater than zero, a miles-per-gallon chip when the stay has miles per gallon, and a price-per-gallon chip when that cost has a price, such as $4.00/gal. Each chip has its own mark. A missing figure hides its chip. It shows a fern miles chip, such as 270 miles, when the stay has driving miles from the previous stay, and hides that chip when it does not. Each stay in that month's list uses its color. Locate on that stay flies the globe to its pin and zooms in to street level, including when that stay is already centered. The pencil opens the editor. The row itself does not move the globe. Night uses Positron and Day uses Voyager. A control on the globe chooses how many past months to draw, from 0 through 12, and it starts at 0. That choice stays while you edit a stay or leave Trips and come back. Sidebar Trips opens again at 0. Zero leaves out past months, and current and future stays stay drawn. Editing a stay uses satellite imagery, zoomed in on the pin. That pin is labeled with the stay name and the street address. Each pin is labeled with the place name and the city and state. The screen opens on today's month and the stay that covers today. When none covers today, it opens the soonest stay that has not ended. Previous steps to the next older stay and does not wrap. Next only goes forward. A drive leg asks the driving router once, and again if that request fails. Pins are joined by that driving line, labeled in miles. A drive with no road route is a dashed short path with no miles and no airplane, and the screen says the road route is unavailable. A fly leg skips that check. It is a dashed short path with a teal airplane at its middle and no miles. The path splits at the date line so the line does not wrap the long way around the map. A fly stay whose departure is still inside an earlier stay on the route also draws a return fly leg back to that stay. A stay nested inside that ongoing stay, or the next campground that starts when the ongoing stay ends, does not get a drive leg from the flight city; the drive leg runs from the ongoing stay instead. When the next stay on the route is a drive, starts before the ongoing stay ends, is not nested in it, and its arrival is on or after the flight departure, the route continues with a drive leg from the flight to that next stay instead. Miles on the leg and in the month list are the driving miles. The month list leads with the stay dates as a month and day, without the year, such as Nov 20 – Nov 24. A span that would read as a different length keeps the year. The place, the note, the city and state as one chip, a Flight chip when travel is fly, the balance due when the amount due is greater than zero, a fuel figure when the cost is greater than zero, and miles are chips when the stay has driving miles from the route. The open stay card shows the same Flight chip when travel is fly. Stay chips on the list and the open card are dark grey with white text and a colored mark so they read on the stay color. Done, underway, and upcoming are not shown as chips. The open stay card shows those miles as a chip and does not repeat them in a sentence. Next and previous fly the globe to that stay in 1000ms at city zoom, halfway between the world zoom and the locate zoom, and show that stay's arrival month on the calendar. A stay already in the visible month leaves the month as it is. The rating for the open stay sits on that same row as Previous and Next. Stars are teal. An empty star is an outline, a chosen star is filled, and a half star fills the left half. Hovering a half ghosts every star from the first through that value at half opacity, a full star for each whole step and only the left half on a half step. The star under the pointer grows slightly and gains a lighter teal glow. Stars past that value stay outlines. After a rating is set, the number shows with one decimal beside a star, from 0.5 through 5.0, and the comment field sits on the same row. On a narrow screen the comment wraps under that row. The comment saves about a second after typing stops. The place name and the note show with that pin. Reduced motion moves the globe without the flight. The open card shows the arrival and departure dates only. Upcoming, underway, and done stay off the screen.

A stay with a fuel estimate keeps one active fuel bill on the Fuel bucket. It draws from the gas favorite. Saving or moving a stay refreshes that bill. The fuel label uses the driving miles from the previous stay on the route and the starred vehicle only. Miles per gallon are the total miles of that vehicle's in-range fill-ups divided by the total gallons of those same fill-ups. A fill-up is every fuel bill on that vehicle with the same odometer. An unpaid fill-up counts the same as a paid one. A fill-up with no miles per gallon is ignored. A fill-up outside the range stays on the fuel list and is left out of the average. When the starred vehicle has no in-range fill-up yet, the label uses the midpoint of its minimum and maximum. The price is the starred vehicle's newest priced fill-up, or that vehicle's fallback price when none is logged, in cents. The open stay shows the miles per gallon and that price, such as $4.00/gal, as chips beside the cost. It does not show a fuel sentence. Cost is the miles divided by that figure, times the price, half up. No starred vehicle, no previous stay, or an empty price means miles only and no cost. The first stay has no cost. The label does not move a balance. The Fuel bucket for the arrival month counts that cost while the stay has not ended. The vehicle page lists one row per fill-up. See [a vehicle is its own odometer](../adr/0019-vehicle-odometer.md).

Demo seed: campground stays from Devils Garden on 2026-09-06 through La Posa South on 2027-01-08. Devils Garden is fly. The other stays are drive. Each stay leaves the morning the next arrives, so every day in that span is on a site, including 2026-11-05 at Usery Mountain. The route follows that arrival order. Lost Dutchman is unrated, with the balance due on 2026-11-22. Cottonwood stores 7 half-stars and a comment. A stay does not change an account balance.

Requires accounts. Lodging does not write a balance. See [a balance is only what you type](../adr/0022-balance-is-only-what-you-type.md).
