# Module 25 — Trips

Status: now

A trip is one place: a name, an optional note, an arrival, a departure, and one pin. A second park is a second trip. Arrival and departure both count as days of the stay. The calendar shows the place on each of those days.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Place name | Required, trimmed, 1–80 characters. The only name |
| Note | Optional, up to 2000 characters. Shown with the name, such as a site number |
| Rating | Optional. An integer count of half-stars. The screen offers 1 through 10, a half star through 5, and shows that number with one decimal beside a star, such as 1.0 or 1.5. Empty means unrated. Saving it does not post |
| Comment | Optional, trimmed, up to 2000 characters. Shown after a rating is set, on the same row as the stars on a wide screen and under them on a narrow one. It saves about a second after typing stops. An empty comment is stored as none. Refused when the stay is unrated |
| Arrival, departure | Calendar dates. Departure is on or after arrival |
| Coordinates | Required. Search, a dragged pin, or typed latitude and longitude |
| Draw-from account | Required when the total is greater than zero. One account for the deposit and the amount due |
| Total | Integer minor units, zero or greater, in the account's currency |
| Amount due | Integer minor units, zero or greater, and not above the total |
| Balance-due date | Optional. Prefilled with arrival. Saving it does not post |

The deposit is total minus amount due. It is not stored. Saving a trip does not move a balance. A zero deposit has nothing to charge. When the deposit is greater than zero, a confirmation shows the old balance and the new one. Apply writes one posting dated today, against a bucket you choose. Skip leaves the deposit uncharged. The amount due stays uncharged until you mark it, with the same confirmation, and a second time is refused. On a checking account the balance falls. On a card the amount owed rises, and checking is not touched. Apply is refused when an asset would go below zero. A card may go over its limit, and the confirmation says so. Editing a charge already applied asks you to confirm the new balance before the posting changes. Deleting a trip asks you to confirm, then reverses its lodging postings. Other postings lose the stay and stay in the books. Archiving an account is refused while a trip drawn from it still has an uncharged deposit or an uncharged amount due.

Two stays may share a handoff date and may not share a night. Overlap is one arriving before the other departs, and the other arriving before the first departs. Create and update both refuse an overlap and name the other stay.

The screen opens on a 3D globe with the month under it. Stays take colors in arrival order: poppy, lagoon, berry, citrus, violet, apricot, tide, blossom, fern, lilac, lemon, sea, then those twelve again, so two stays next to each other do not share a color. A day between arrival and departure is filled with that color. Arrival is the upper-right half of a split from the top-left corner to the bottom-right. Departure is the lower-left half. A shared handoff date shows both. Clicking that day selects the stay that is arriving. The open stay is a card in its color. That card shows a sky balance-due chip when the stay has a balance-due date: the scale mark, the amount due in money, and the date as a short month and day, such as $120.00 Due Nov 22. A zero amount still shows as $0.00. The chip stays hidden when the stay has no balance-due date. It shows an amber fuel-amount chip, labeled Fuel and the cost, when the stay has a fuel cost, a violet miles-per-gallon chip when the stay has miles per gallon, and a coral price-per-gallon chip when that cost has a price, such as $4.00/gal. Each chip has its own mark. A missing figure hides its chip. It shows a fern miles chip, such as 270 miles, when the stay has driving miles from the previous stay, and hides that chip when it does not. Each stay in that month's list uses its color. The dark theme uses Dark Matter and the light theme uses Positron. Editing a stay uses satellite imagery, zoomed in on the pin. That pin is labeled with the stay name and the street address. Each pin is labeled with its stay number in arrival order and the city and state, such as Stay 1 and Las Vegas, NV. Every leg asks the driving router once, and again if that request fails. Pins are joined by that driving line, labeled in miles. A missing line stays missing, and the screen says the road route is unavailable. Miles on the leg and in the month list are the driving miles. The month list says how many miles it is from the previous stay. The open stay card shows those miles as a chip and does not repeat them in a sentence. Next and previous fly the globe to the pin in 400ms and show that stay's arrival month on the calendar. A stay already in the visible month leaves the month as it is. The rating for the open stay sits on that same row, beside Edit. Stars are teal. An empty star is an outline, a chosen star is filled, and a half star fills the left half. Hovering a half ghosts every star from the first through that value at half opacity, a full star for each whole step and only the left half on a half step. The star under the pointer grows slightly and gains a lighter teal glow. Stars past that value stay outlines. After a rating is set, the number shows with one decimal beside a star, from 0.5 through 5.0, and the comment field sits on the same row. On a narrow screen the comment wraps under that row. The comment saves about a second after typing stops. The place name and the note show with that pin. Reduced motion moves the globe without the flight. Status is upcoming, underway, or done from the dates and today.

The fuel label uses the driving miles from the previous stay and the starred vehicle only. Miles per gallon are the total miles of that vehicle's in-range stretches divided by the total gallons of those same stretches, including the endpoints of its minimum and maximum. A stretch with no miles per gallon is ignored. A stretch outside the range stays on the fuel log and is left out of the average. When the starred vehicle has no in-range stretch yet, the label uses the midpoint of its minimum and maximum. The price is the fallback fuel price, edited in Settings, in base-currency minor units. The open stay shows the miles per gallon and that price, such as $4.00/gal, as chips beside the cost. It does not show a fuel sentence. Cost is the miles divided by that figure, times the price, half up. No starred vehicle, no previous stay, or an empty price means miles only and no cost. The first stay has no cost. The label assigns nothing and does not move a balance. The log still shows the instantaneous stretch. See [a vehicle is its own odometer](../adr/0019-vehicle-odometer.md).

Demo seed: Stay 1 in Las Vegas, NV, with the whole price due on arrival, and Stay 2 in Los Angeles, CA, with a deposit, a note, and one applied lodging charge. One stay stores a balance-due date. Stay 2 stores 7 half-stars and Comment 1. Stay 1 is unrated.

Requires accounts and buckets. The lodging posting is the first posting. [A confirmed posting moves the stored balance](../adr/0016-posting-moves-balance.md).
