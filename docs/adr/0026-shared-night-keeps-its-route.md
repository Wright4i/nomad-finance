# A shared night keeps its route

A stay can arrive while another stay is still going, including one night at the end of a longer stay. The calendar shows that arrival night beside the stay already there, and any further arrivals on that day are chosen from the list. The driving line and the fuel miles follow a route. When the later stay ends after the earlier one, that later end is already second. When a stay starts and ends during the one beside it, Earlier and Later trade those two, and they do not jump a stay on another day.
