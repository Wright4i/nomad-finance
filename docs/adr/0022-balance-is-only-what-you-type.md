# A balance is only what you type

Supersedes [0016](0016-posting-moves-balance.md).

Nothing writes `accounts.balance_minor` except typing the balance. Postings and splits are gone. A bill, including a paid fuel bill, does not move the account. Lodging total, deposit, and amount due stay on the stay and do not charge the account. Logged fuel is an unpaid one-time fuel bill with a vehicle, an odometer, and a price per gallon, so a later fill-up can use that history. Mark paid comes later and does not move the account. A fill-up is every fuel bill on one vehicle with the same odometer, unpaid or paid. Miles are that reading minus the previous different reading. Gallons add each charge. A missing price hides miles per gallon.
