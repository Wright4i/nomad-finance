# Average payroll

The 3-to-6 month window on hourly pay is retired. A pay cycle has one checkbox, average payroll, off by default, on any pay kind. A payroll entry is one dollar amount on one payday. A keyed amount always wins. When the checkbox is on and a payday has no entry, that payday shows the average of keyed amounts dated before it, half up. Otherwise the payday shows the paycheck from the rate. An empty amount is not keyed. A keyed irregular date shows on the calendar and counts in the average, and it does not change the stored next payday.
