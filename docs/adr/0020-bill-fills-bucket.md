# A bill fills its bucket

A bill used to name a bucket as a label and leave the envelope alone. Saving or moving a bill now writes that amount, converted to the base currency, into the bucket on the oldest active pay cycle. Mark paid still does not move an account balance, and it does not rewrite the envelope. The other choices were to keep the label, or to measure the gauges against bills instead of assigned cash. Filling the assignment keeps the 50/30/20 gauges and puts every bill inside a group.
