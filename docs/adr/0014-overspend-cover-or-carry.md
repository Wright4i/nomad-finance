# A negative assignment is covered now or carried

An assignment can be negative. That is overspending, decided here after [one cash pool](0012-one-pool-across-payors.md) left it open. Each category stores the choice. Cover now is the default: while the period is open, the negative amount is pulled from available-to-assign, and it does not enter the next period. Carry leaves available-to-assign alone and brings the negative amount into the next period. A positive amount enters the next period either way, so unspent cash stays in the category. The closed period keeps the amount it had.
