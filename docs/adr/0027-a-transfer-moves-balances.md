# A transfer moves balances

Supersedes [0022](0022-balance-is-only-what-you-type.md).

A transfer is the one action that writes two account balances without typing either one. Posting stays retired. A bill still does not move a balance by itself, except a new log drawn from a liability, which adds that amount to the amount owed. Mark paid on the ordinary bill path leaves the balance as it was. Paying a paid-in-full card from the transfer screen is the path that both moves the cash and marks the selected bills paid. The amount is the full payment. It leaves the source account and it lowers the card by that same amount. Selecting a bucket does not move cash. The part of the payment those bills do not cover can be added to the card's water on the screen. That click does not change the payment. It marks that bill paid when the payment is confirmed, and that bucket is lowered by the bill, so the assigned money is not counted twice. The selected bills cannot be more than the payment. The part of the payment past those bills is still cash from the source. Available to assign stays the same when the bills equal the payment. Cash beyond those bills is still the same payment, not a second move. Neither balance goes below zero. The payment is at least one cent and no more than the card's balance or the source balance. Paying a card can be a partial payment of the balance. The card's water is the selected buckets filling toward that payment. A bucket on that screen is an unpaid bill drawn from the card and due today or older. A bill due today is included. A future due date is not.

An in-budget asset moved to another in-budget asset changes those two balances and leaves available-to-assign and the buckets alone. A transfer between asset accounts is allowed when the amount is at least one cent and no more than the source account's balance. The only Short is the amount asked for beyond that balance. Unassigned cash, buckets, and leaving Include in Budget do not refuse the transfer. A transfer that leaves Include in Budget takes unassigned cash first. Bucket money may be taken after that, and it does not wait to be accepted. A locked bucket is not pulled below its bills. When the buckets do not cover the rest, the two balances still move.
