# The envelope is one amount

The amount typed in Envelope is one number per bucket. Changing it on a current or later period changes it on every period, including a closed period, which stays view only. Auto-assigned is the only amount that differs between accounting periods, and it is the bills in that period, including trip fuel on Fuel. A goal, a goal with no target, and a locked bucket follow the same rule.

This replaces the part of [a month starts empty](0025-a-month-starts-empty.md) that dropped the typed amount, and the part of [an assignment stays at zero or more](0024-assignments-stay-zero-or-more.md) that kept a separate amount on a closed period. The envelope still cannot go below zero.
