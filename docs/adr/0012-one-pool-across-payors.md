# One cash pool across payors

Each payor already has its own period. Available-to-assign stays one number for the books. An assignment is stored on one payor's period, and that same total covers all of them. A closed period keeps its rows. A new month starts empty, apart from the bills due then. A pool per payor would split cash the accounts do not split. Rollover, later, decides what a negative assignment does. Until then an assignment is zero or more.

Available to assign on the Budget screen is income still coming in this month, plus every asset account except savings, minus what the buckets already hold. A payday before today is left out. Savings accounts stay out. It is not one payor's paycheck, and it does not follow a selected payor or a closed month. The line under that number is what is already assigned of the income still coming in plus those accounts. Needs, Wants, and Savings targets are 50%, 30%, and 20% of that income plus those accounts, half up to the cent. A goal assignment is still stored on one payor's period and counted once in the month, and it lowers the number left to assign. A locked bucket's bills are counted once for the month, not once per payor. Bucket maximums stay on the tiles and do not reduce this total.
