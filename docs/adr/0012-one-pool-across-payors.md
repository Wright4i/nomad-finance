# One cash pool across payors

Each payor already has its own period. Available-to-assign stays one number for the books. An assignment is stored on one payor's period, and that same total covers all of them. A closed period keeps its rows. When the next period opens, that payor's assignments carry in. A pool per payor would split cash the accounts do not split. Rollover, later, decides what a negative assignment does. Until then an assignment is zero or more.

Available to assign on the envelopes screen is the income from every payor in the open month plus checking, savings, and cash. It is not one payor's paycheck, and it does not follow a selected payor. Needs, Wants, and Savings targets are 50%, 30%, and 20% of that total, half up to the cent. A goal assignment is still stored on one payor's period and counted once in the month. A locked bucket's bills are counted once for the month, not once per payor. Bucket maximums stay on the tiles and do not reduce this total.
