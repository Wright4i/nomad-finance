# The calendar horizon assigns nothing

The books stay a pay-period register. The calendar is a view over those periods. The horizon is 30 or 60 days, today through the next 29 or 59 days, saved on the user, and the default is 60. It assigns nothing. Plans and the Credit picture are dropped. Previews and the fuel label do not move a stored balance or available-to-assign. This keeps docs/adr/0016-posting-moves-balance.md ([a confirmed posting moves the stored balance](0016-posting-moves-balance.md)).
